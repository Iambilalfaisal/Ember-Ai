# Pricing

Brightwave has three plans: Starter, Team and Enterprise.

The Starter plan costs 12 dollars per user per month and includes up to 5 users.

The Team plan costs 29 dollars per user per month and adds shared dashboards and priority email support.

The Enterprise plan has custom pricing and adds single sign-on, a dedicated account manager and a 99.9 percent uptime guarantee.

Annual billing gives a 15 percent discount on every plan. Non-profit organisations receive a 30 percent discount.

All plans include a 14-day free trial. No credit card is needed to start the trial.
